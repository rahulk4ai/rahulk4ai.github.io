AI Career Radar

AI Career Radar is a private personal job-discovery and career-research application operated for its account owner. It is not a public job board or a service accepting public sign-ups.

The application organizes job observations, career-policy evaluations and research reports. With its owner's authorization, it creates and updates its own private Google Drive files, Google Sheets workbooks and, when needed, Google Docs documents.

Google access is limited to files created by the application or explicitly opened or selected for it. The owner can inspect and revoke that access from their Google Account. There is no Gmail access in this authorization.

This public website provides application information for Google account consent. It contains no workbook, résumé, job records, credentials or private application source code.

Privacy policy

Operated by the dedicated AI Career Radar account owner. Support contact is the owner contact shown on the application's Google consent screen.
